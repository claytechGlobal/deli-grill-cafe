What we sell

Made-to-order food (breakfast and hot sandwiches, burgers, platters over rice, sides), Boar’s Head cold cuts and cheese by the pound, baked goods, and coffee and tea, sold for in-store pickup.

Prices

All prices are in U.S. dollars (USD) and do not include applicable New York sales tax, which is added at checkout. Where two prices are shown, the card price is about 4% higher than the cash price. Prices and menu items can change without notice; the price confirmed at checkout is the price you pay.

Orders

An order is accepted once you receive a confirmation. We may decline or cancel an order — for example, if an item is unavailable or a price was listed in error — and will fully refund any payment for it.

Our kitchen handles eggs, milk, wheat, soy, fish, sesame, peanuts and tree nuts. We can’t guarantee any item is free of allergens. Please tell us about allergies before you order.

If any part of these terms is found unenforceable, the rest stays in effect. Questions: call 718-567-2294 or email info@elegantedeli.com.

Wrong or unsatisfactory orders

If your order is wrong, missing an item, or not prepared properly, tell us within 24 hours of pickup — in person, by phone at 718-567-2294, or by email at info@elegantedeli.com. We’ll remake the item or refund it to your original payment method, whichever you prefer.

Cancellations

You can cancel for a full refund until we start preparing your order. Because food is made fresh to order, orders that are already being prepared or are ready for pickup can’t be cancelled. Orders not picked up within 2 hours of the ready time are not refunded.

Packaged goods & deli by the pound

Unopened packaged items can be returned within 24 hours with a receipt. For food-safety reasons, sliced meats and cheeses can’t be returned once they leave the store unless they were sliced or weighed incorrectly.

How refunds are processed

Approved card refunds are issued to the original card within 2 business days; your bank may take 5–10 business days to post them. Cash purchases are refunded in cash. EBT purchases are refunded to the EBT card.

Orders are picked up at the counter at 5304 4th Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11220. We’re open 24 hours, 7 days a week. Most orders are ready in 10–20 minutes; we’ll tell you if it will take longer.

We don’t ship. Delivery orders placed through third-party apps are delivered by those services and also follow their policies; for a problem with the food itself, contact us directly and we’ll make it right under our refund policy.

What we collect

When you order or contact us we may collect your name, phone number, email address, and order details. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Payments

Card payments are processed by our payment processor (such as Stripe). We never see or store your full card number.

How we use it

Only to prepare and fulfill your order, contact you about it, handle refunds, and keep the records the law requires. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, email info@elegantedeli.com or call 718-567-2294.

Online card payments are handled over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection by a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor. In store we accept cash, credit and debit cards, and EBT.

EBT: under SNAP rules, EBT cannot be used for hot or prepared food. It can be used for eligible cold groceries, such as deli meats and cheese by the pound.

Age-restricted items: a valid photo ID is required for any age-restricted product sold in store, and these items are not sold online.

Promotions: any discount or special lists its terms and end date where it is offered, can’t be combined with other offers unless stated, and has no cash value.
